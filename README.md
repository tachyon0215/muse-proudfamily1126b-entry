# muse-proudfamily1126b-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene](https://make.fun/bounty/u-shino-make-the-ai-judge-feel-proud-a-family-scene-ee2e3fa2).

## The entry

> Dad spent all morning teaching the baby to say 'mama' first. She looked at him, grinned, and said it - to the dog. He clapped anyway, loudest of all.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: the turn (her first 'mama' goes to the dog) is the light recognisable laugh, and the last clause is pure affection - he lost his own contest and clapped loudest anyway.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
